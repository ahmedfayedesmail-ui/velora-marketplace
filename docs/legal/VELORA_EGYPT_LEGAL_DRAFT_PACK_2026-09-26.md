## Legal basis reviewed for this draft
This pack is designed around:
1. Egypt Consumer Protection Law No. 181/2018, including remote-contract rules and later amendment by Law No. 20/2024.
2. Executive Regulations of the Consumer Protection Law, Decision No. 822/2019.
3. Egypt Personal Data Protection Law No. 151/2020.
4. Executive Regulations of the Personal Data Protection Law, Ministerial Decision No. 816/2025.
5. Electronic Signature Law No. 15/2004 and its regulatory framework.
6. Cybercrime Law No. 175/2018.
7. Competition Law No. 3/2005 and other sector-specific rules where applicable.

The Consumer Protection Law requires clear pre-contract information for remote sales and provides statutory rights that cannot simply be waived by contract. The remote-sale framework includes seller identification, product information, confirmation/correction mechanics, and a 14-day withdrawal right subject to statutory exceptions. The law also requires invoices and prohibits misleading conduct.

The 2025 executive regulations for the Personal Data Protection Law require lawful collection/processing, clear purpose, security, retention controls, records, mechanisms for data-subject rights, and privacy governance.

# 1. Customer Terms of Use / Sale
Velora shall identify the actual contracting seller for each purchase and shall clearly state Velora's legally confirmed marketplace role. The terms shall not use a blanket disclaimer to avoid mandatory consumer obligations.

Before order formation, the customer shall see seller identity/contact details, applicable registration/tax information, manufacturer/importer information where relevant, product source and essential characteristics, instructions and warnings, price and additional charges, shipping/delivery information, warranty/after-sales information, applicable return rules, payment method and material promotion restrictions.

The checkout shall give the customer an opportunity to review and correct order data before final submission, and the final order record shall preserve the material purchase information shown at formation.

Nothing in the terms limits mandatory consumer rights concerning conformity, defects, warranty, withdrawal/returns, delivery, refunds or complaints.

---

# 2. Privacy Policy
Velora may process account, authentication, contact, order/shipping, support, seller-business, security/device and Beauty Passport information only for stated lawful purposes.

Purposes may include account services, orders, payment, fulfillment, fraud/security, support, seller onboarding, personalization, legal/tax/audit obligations, and lawful analytics. Marketing communications shall use the applicable legal basis and consent/permission where required.

The policy shall disclose categories of recipients and processors, cross-border access/transfer where applicable, retention rules, security controls, incident handling, and data-subject rights.

The final implementation shall identify controller/processor roles, privacy responsibility/DPO requirements, processor contracts, processing records, retention schedule, and required licenses/permits.

---

# 3. Returns & Refunds Policy
The policy shall distinguish:
1. statutory remote-sale withdrawal rights;
2. defective/non-conforming goods;
3. seller/contractual return rights;
4. voluntary goodwill.

For remote contracts, the statutory withdrawal period is generally 14 days from receipt, subject to statutory exceptions. Defective/non-conforming goods have separate legal remedies, including the statutory 30-day period described in Article 21. Refund mechanics shall follow applicable law and payment rules.

Velora shall not impose a blanket "all cosmetics are non-returnable" rule. Any product-specific exception must be mapped to applicable law and approved by counsel.

Every return/refund request shall preserve item, dates, reason, evidence, seller, eligibility decision, shipping responsibility, refund amount/method, and audit trail.

---

# 4. Shipping & Delivery Policy
Before checkout the customer shall see the applicable delivery method, charge and estimate when available.

The final policy shall define delivery areas, estimates, failed delivery, loss/damage allocation, tracking, delivery proof, return-to-sender, delayed-delivery rights, and responsibility between seller, carrier and Velora according to the final legal structure.

The current Restore-Test carrier is manual and shall never be described as an external carrier integration.

---

# 5. Cancellation Policy
Cancellation before dispatch shall follow the final operational rules.

Cancellation shall not remove statutory remote-sale rights or rights relating to defective/non-conforming goods.

The system shall preserve request time, order status, eligibility, decision, reason and refund status.

---

# 6. Payment Terms
Only payment routes operationally enabled for the relevant country/currency/environment may be displayed as available.

The current Restore-Test evidence supports a test Card/Paymob route and Cash on Delivery route. This is not evidence of live settlement.

Velora shall not store raw card credentials. Payment status, attempts, provider references, refunds and reconciliation shall be server-authoritative and idempotent.

---

# 7. Promotions / Coupons / Gift Cards Terms
Each offer shall clearly state issuer, eligibility, start/end, discount mechanism, minimum order, maximum discount, usage limits, exclusions, stacking rules, refund/reversal treatment and any other material restriction.

Discount claims shall be truthful and shall not create a false impression of a price reduction.

Gift-card issuance, expiry, refund, consumer-rights and tax/accounting rules shall be confirmed before publication.

---

# 8. Seller Terms / Marketplace Agreement
Every seller shall provide truthful identity/business information and comply with all laws applicable to its products and activities.

Seller obligations shall include lawful registration where applicable, accurate product data, authenticity, inventory accuracy, lawful pricing/advertising, fulfillment, consumer rights, complaints cooperation, prohibited-product restrictions, evidence retention and tax/invoice compliance.

The agreement shall not attempt to contract away mandatory consumer or data-protection obligations.

# 11. Payout Terms
Payouts require verified payee identity, valid destination, eligible balance, reconciliation, completion of applicable holds, and no unresolved blocking conditions under the approved contract.

# 12. Fulfillment Policy
Sellers shall prepare and hand over orders within the applicable SLA.

The system shall record order item, seller/store, shipment/service, tracking reference, status transitions, failed delivery, delivery proof where applicable and return-to-sender.

---

# 13. Product Authenticity / Seller Verification Policy
Seller verification and product authenticity are distinct controls.

Sellers shall be responsible for authenticity and lawful sourcing as required by law and contract. Velora may request invoices, authorization evidence, supplier records, batch/lot details, expiry information or manufacturer/importer evidence where relevant.

Velora may suspend listings for investigation where legally justified and shall preserve evidence of the investigation and resolution.

---

# 14. Prohibited Products / Catalog Policy
At minimum, prohibited or restricted products shall include unlawful goods, counterfeit or stolen goods, unsafe products, products requiring an authorization the seller does not hold, misleading products/claims, prohibited medical/drug claims, products violating intellectual-property rights, and any other products prohibited by applicable law or documented platform policy.

Category-specific restrictions shall be expanded as the marketplace grows.

---

# 15. Review Policy
Reviews must reflect genuine customer experience and may not be fabricated, purchased or manipulated.

Velora may remove unlawful, fraudulent, abusive, privacy-invasive, irrelevant or otherwise prohibited content under a documented moderation process with an audit trail.

---

# 16. Beauty Advisor / AI Disclaimer
Velora's Beauty Passport and routine engine provide cosmetic shopping and personalization guidance only.

They are not medical diagnosis, treatment instructions, a dermatologist assessment, a guarantee of outcome, or a substitute for qualified medical or pharmacy advice.

Users should follow manufacturer instructions and warnings and seek qualified care for medical conditions, adverse reactions, pregnancy-related questions, medication interactions, severe/persistent symptoms or other health concerns.

The routine engine shall not invent product facts or health claims.

---

# 17. Data Retention Policy
Retention shall be defined by category and purpose, including account/security data, orders/invoice/payment evidence, seller onboarding evidence, legal acceptances, support/complaints, security logs, marketing consent, Beauty Passport information and analytics/telemetry.

Data shall not be retained in identifiable form longer than necessary for the lawful purpose, except where law requires retention for a defined period. Where retention is legally required after the original purpose, appropriate protection and de-identification measures shall be applied where permitted.

---

# 18. Dispute / Chargeback / Complaints Process
The platform shall expose a customer support/complaints channel and maintain:
Complaint → Evidence → Seller/Operations Review → Decision → Remedy/Refund → Escalation.

Nothing in the process prevents statutory complaints to competent authorities.

Chargebacks shall be handled under the provider's rules and applicable law, with synchronized internal records.

---

# 19. Cookie / Tracking Notice
The final notice shall identify necessary storage, analytics, marketing, personalization and relevant third parties.

Non-essential tracking shall not be bundled into mandatory service acceptance where separate consent is legally required.

The notice shall state retention and the available control/opt-out mechanism where applicable.

---

# 20. Copyright / IP / Takedown & Legal Notice
Sellers shall have the rights necessary to use product names, images, descriptions, trademarks and submitted content.

The takedown process shall capture claimant identity/contact, evidence of rights, exact material/listing, requested remedy and escalation/counter-notice handling where appropriate.

The Legal Notice shall identify the real operating legal entity and legally required registration/contact information before publication.
