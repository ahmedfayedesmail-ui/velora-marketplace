# Velora — SELLER TARGETS
## Public prospect list — 2026-09-21

Purpose: identify 10 qualified Egyptian beauty/skincare businesses suitable for a small founding-seller experiment.

This is a prospecting list, not a claim that any seller has agreed to join Velora. Outreach status starts as NOT CONTACTED.

## Selection criteria

Target sellers should have:
- real beauty/skincare products;
- an existing direct-to-consumer path;
- public Instagram and/or WhatsApp contact;
- enough product variety to test matching;
- a plausible “customer is unsure what to buy” use case.
